## 1. What is Caprae's mission?

Caprae's mission is to turn good businesses into great ones by creating value after the acquisition, not through financial engineering. It treats M&A as a seven-year journey and focuses on the operational and AI-driven transformation that follows the deal.

It does this in two ways. It helps searchers, independent sponsors and PE firms find and close off-market deals, through services like Search as a Service. It also builds its own technology, such as SaaSquatch, so owners and operators can grow faster with AI. Underneath it all is a belief that founders and operators with character, courage and creativity build great companies.

## 2. Why do you want to work at Caprae Capital?

I want to build software that directly shapes real business decisions. At Caprae, a tool like SaaSquatch decides which owners a searcher calls and how much time and money a deal takes. The handbook challenge made this concrete: I built Deal Radar, which ranks leads by how likely the owner is to sell and explains why, before any credit is spent. That felt like work I'd want to do every day.

I also fit how Caprae works: it builds its own tools, moves fast and expects high ownership. [Add one example of a project you owned end to end.] I want to grow somewhere engineers learn the business, not just the codebase, and Caprae sits exactly where private equity, operations and AI meet.

## 3. How is Caprae changing the ETA space and broader PE?

**In ETA**, Caprae makes the search cheaper and less lonely. Instead of one person spending years sourcing deals with expensive tools, Caprae offers search, outreach and post-acquisition support as a service, paid partly on success. SaaSquatch replaces a stack of tools costing over $1,000 a month with one priced around $20, and it reached 1,200+ sign-ups in a three-week beta.

**In broader PE**, Caprae shifts value creation from spreadsheets to operations: AI-readiness, technology and post-acquisition strategy instead of leverage and multiple expansion. It also changes who does the work, hiring for "horsepower over mileage" and training a new generation of more technical, founder-minded investors and operators.
